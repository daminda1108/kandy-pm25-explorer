How it works

Each hour the valley's air is built from two parts. A regional background arrives from outside, carried by the monsoon winds over the whole island. On top of it the valley adds its own: traffic, cooking, burning, held in when the air above the ridges stops mixing.

How much, and when

The hour-to-hour total comes from a machine-learning model trained on two low-cost sensors near Kandy and driven by weather and a global chemistry forecast. Its yearly level comes from satellite estimates, scaled to the Central Environmental Authority's Kandy monitoring station; its daily cycle is fitted to that station too. Checked on later station years (2022–2023) the level is about right on average (about 1 % low, with wide scatter from day to day); day to day it does about as well as a scaled global reanalysis.

Where in the valley

Nothing measures how the air differs between Kandy's towns: the valley has one regulatory station. The map's pattern is physics, not measurement: a satellite surface, stronger where air is trapped under the ridges. How strong that effect is cannot be pinned down, so the Where view shows the whole family of patterns the evidence allows and colours only what they all agree on. In valley cities that do have monitoring networks, two places 3–10 km apart usually differ by about 13 % in their long-run PM2.5; models learned there could not say which of two such places is dirtier, and maps of roads, buildings and population could not say which places differ more.

Three kinds of year

2019–2023 are anchored to the satellite level and the station. 2024–2026 are an extension modelled from weather and chemistry: the level there reads about 40 % low against the station, so treat it as uncertain. The forecast is a demonstration: a forecast level with a typical pattern for that hour.

The range

Every number comes with a 90 % range, widened after checking against the station so that it holds about nine hours in ten.

Colours

Colours are fixed to World Health Organization values (2021): 5 µg/m³ is the annual guideline, 15 the 24-hour guideline, 35 and 75 interim targets. A colour always means the same concentration.
